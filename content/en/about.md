Hi! I'm Şükrü Çakmak, a computer engineer who has been developing software for over 20 years. Most of my career has been spent in the **Java** and **Spring** ecosystem, working as a developer, architect, and consultant on enterprise-scale projects.

On this blog I write about Java, Spring, software architecture, and community experiences. Some posts are also published in Turkish — use the language switcher in the top right to switch.

You can reach me on [GitHub](https://github.com/sukrucakmak).
